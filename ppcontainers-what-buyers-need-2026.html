The Complete Guide to PP Containers

Polypropylene corrugated PP containers represent the most significant upgrade available to procurement teams still running single-use cardboard. The material is waterproof, structurally consistent, forklift-compatible, and designed for 50-100 reuse cycles. For industries in food processing, industrial, bulk storage, the switch from cardboard to PP corrugated typically pays for itself within one production season.

This guide covers everything buyers need to know about sourcing, specifying, and getting maximum value from PP containers: material specs, industry applications, import duty considerations, and how to evaluate suppliers in a market full of resellers moving low-quality imports.

Specification Guide for PP Containers

Choosing the right PP containers requires matching wall thickness, flute profile, and resin grade to your specific application. Key specifications:

  • Wall thickness: 4mm for light duty, 6mm for standard, 8mm for heavy duty, 10mm for bulk container applications
  • Flute type: B-flute for top-load stacking strength, C-flute for balanced performance, X-flute (double wall) for maximum rigidity
  • Resin grade: Standard PP for ambient environments, high-density PP for cold chain and freezer applications down to -40°F
  • Food-grade: FDA 21 CFR-compliant virgin PP for direct food contact — required for produce, poultry, seafood, and processed food applications
  • Custom dimensions: Elipacko cuts to custom sizes with lead times varying by volume — contact directly for specifications
